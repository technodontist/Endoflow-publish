## 5. CLINICAL PROCEDURE SECTION (Pages 22-26)

### **Step-by-Step Protocol** (detailed narrative):

1. **Pre-operative Preparation**:
   - 10 ml 0.2% chlorhexidine digluconate rinse
   - Inferior alveolar nerve block (IANB) - Lox 2%, Neon Lab
   - EPT after 15 minutes (Sybron Endo Vitality Scanner)

2. **Pre-operative Occlusal Analysis**:
   - T-Scan NOVUS recording
   - Percentage of relative occlusal force in centric occlusion
   - Right and left lateral excursion measurements
   - Multiple repetitions, average value recorded

3. **Access and Pulp Removal**:
   - Rubber dam isolation
   - Carious lesion removal
   - Missing proximal wall restoration with composite
   - Access with diamond bur (#1013, Microdont, high speed, air-water coolant)
   - Visual inspection for pulp bleeding (vitality confirmation)
   - Coronal pulp removal to canal orifice level

4. **Haemostasis Achievement**:
   - 2.5% sodium hypochlorite cotton pellet
   - 2-minute application, repeated up to 4 times
   - **Time to haemostasis recorded**
   - **If >8 minutes**: Case excluded, pulpectomy initiated

5. **Biodentine Application**:
   - Mixed per manufacturer instructions
   - 2-3 mm layer above pulp tissue
   - MAP system placement
   - Gentle condensing

6. **Final Restoration**:
   - RMGIC (Vitrebond, 3M ESPE) layer, light-cured 20 seconds
   - Resin composite (Tetric N-Ceram Bulk fill) restoration
   - Rubber dam removal
   - Finishing and polishing (Shofu Dental Corporation points)
   - Intraoral periapical radiograph taken

### **RANDOMIZATION INTO GROUPS** (occurs AFTER restoration, before occlusal management)

**GROUP 1: Full Pulpotomy WITH Occlusal Reduction (TEST GROUP)**
- Selective grinding with wheel diamond burs (MANI, Japan)
- Constant air-water coolant
- Goal: 0% post-intervention relative occlusal force
- T-Scan v10 real-time monitoring showing decreased relative occlusal force percentage
- 6-month recall interval for definitive prosthetic rehabilitation

**GROUP 2: Full Pulpotomy WITHOUT Occlusal Reduction (CONTROL GROUP)**
- Sham procedure: High-speed handpiece activated inside mouth without tooth contact
- Air-water coolant activated (mimics treatment)
- Post-operative T-Scan scan confirming NO change in relative occlusal force percentage

**POST-OPERATIVE MEDICATION**:
- 10 tablets Ibuprofen 600mg prescribed
- Advised to take "when experiencing moderate-severe post-operative pain after consulting doctor"
