## PROTOCOL BODY SECTIONS

### INTRODUCTION (1-4 pages)
**Essential Content**:
- Definition of irreversible pulpitis
- Mechanical allodynia and percussion sensitivity statistics
- Post-operative pain prevalence (3-58% range)
- Vital pulp therapy (VPT) advantages
- Occlusal reduction mechanism
- Research gap identification
- Working hypothesis

**Format**: Narrative paragraphs with superscript citations (ref 1-37)

### REVIEW OF LITERATURE (5-11 pages)
**Two Major Subsections**:

1. **Effects of occlusal reduction on post-operative pain**
   - Cite: Jostes, Creech, Rosenberg, Parirokh, Arslan, Emara, Ahmed, Nguyen-Nhon, Shamszadeh
   - Include: T-Scan technology advantages vs articulating paper discussion

2. **Comparison of pulpotomy vs NSET outcomes**
   - Cite: Taha (multiple years), Eghbal, McHugh, Shallal-Ayzin, Alhilou, Taha & Al-Khatib
   - Include: Success rates, pain relief timelines, patient satisfaction

**Total Citations Required**: 30-40 (Nisarg's protocol uses 37)

### RESEARCH QUESTION
**Format**: Single direct question about intervention effect
**Example**: "Does Occlusal reduction have an effect on post operative pain and outcome of full pulpotomy..."

### LACUNAE IN LITERATURE
**Content**: 1-2 sentences identifying specific research gap this study addresses

### HYPOTHESIS
**Format**: Null hypothesis statement (No difference between groups)

### AIM & OBJECTIVES
**AIM** (underlined): Single goal statement

**PRIMARY OBJECTIVES** (underlined):
1. Primary outcome measurement (with/without intervention)
2. Comparison between groups

**SECONDARY OBJECTIVES** (underlined):
1-4. Additional measured outcomes and comparisons

## METHODOLOGY DETAILS

### SAMPLE SIZE CALCULATION
**Required Information**:
- [ ] Based on: Previous study (author, year)
- [ ] Primary outcome value (mean ± SD)
- [ ] Effect size calculation (method shown)
- [ ] Alpha level (α)
- [ ] Power percentage
- [ ] Number of follow-up points
- [ ] Dropout rate (typically 10%)
- [ ] Final sample size (formula: e.g., n=40 per group, total=80)
- [ ] Software used (e.g., G-Power v3.1.9.4)

### RANDOMIZATION
- [ ] Method specified (variable block randomization described)
- [ ] Block sizes listed (e.g., 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14)
- [ ] Software used (e.g., Stata)
- [ ] Allocation ratio stated (1:1 balance)

### INCLUSION CRITERIA (Bulleted List)
**Must Include**:
- Systemic health status (ASA I & II, age range)
- Tooth characteristics (tooth type, caries depth, vitality tests)
- Diagnosis confirmation (radiographic and clinical)
- Patient commitment (willingness to follow protocol)

### EXCLUSION CRITERIA (Bulleted List)
**Must Include**:
- Contraindicated medications/allergies
- Problematic patient factors (pregnancy, bruxism)
- Tooth-specific exclusions (pre-mature contact, mobility)

### INTRA-OPERATIVE EXCLUSION CRITERIA
**Must Include**:
- Clinical findings that prevent completion (inadequate haemostasis, changed diagnosis)
- Time limits for procedures (e.g., 8-minute haemostasis requirement)

## EVALUATION CRITERIA

### PAIN ASSESSMENT
**Timing**: Pre-operative (baseline) + post-operative (6h, 12h, 24h, daily x7 days, recall visits)

**Scale**: 11-point Numeric Rating Scale (NRS)
- 0 = No pain
- 10 = Worst pain imaginable

**Format**: Include visual scale image

### PAIN CHARACTERISTICS TABLE
| Nature | Quality | Onset | Location | Duration | Triggers | Relief |
|--------|---------|-------|----------|----------|----------|--------|
| None/Mild | Dull | Stimulation | Localized | Sec/Min | Cold/Heat | Cold |
| Mild | Sharp | Intermittent | Diffuse | Hours | Sweet | Heat |
| Moderate | Throbbing | Spontaneous | Radiating | Constant | Spontaneous | Meds |
| Severe | Constant | - | - | - | Posture | - |

### PERCUSSION SENSITIVITY
**Recording**: 11-point NRS (same as pain scale)
**Timing**: Pre-operative + 1st day + 7th day post-operative

### RADIOGRAPHIC ASSESSMENT
- [ ] Equipment specifications (X-ray unit model)
- [ ] Exposure settings (kV, mA, time)
- [ ] Sensor type and positioning device
- [ ] Bite block storage conditions
- [ ] Image software and export format
- [ ] ALARA principle adherence

### PERIAPICAL INDEX (PAI) SCORING
| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Normal periapical structure |
| 2 | Small change in bone structure |
| 3 | Change in bone structure + mineral loss |
| 4 | Periodontitis with radiolucent area |
| 5 | Severe periodontitis with features |

### OCCLUSAL ANALYSIS
**Device**: T-Scan NOVUS (specify handpiece, sensor, software version)
**Measurement**: Percentage of relative occlusal force (pre-operative, post-operative by group)
**Timing**: Pre-treatment, immediately after procedure, at recalls if applicable

## CLINICAL PROCEDURE PROTOCOL

### PRE-OPERATIVE STEPS
1. Rinse: 0.2% chlorhexidine digluconate (10 ml)
2. Anesthesia: IANB with specified local anesthetic
3. EPT confirmation: After 15 minutes, negative response confirms block
4. Occlusal scan: T-Scan NOVUS recording (average of multiple scans)

### OPERATIVE STEPS
1. Rubber dam isolation
2. Caries removal
3. Proximal wall restoration (if needed)
4. Access opening (specify bur: round diamond, size, speed)
5. Pulp removal to canal orifice level
6. Haemostasis with 2.5% NaOCl (record time to achieve)
7. Biomaterial application (Biodentine: 2-3mm layer, specify placement method)
8. Intermediate layer (RMGIC, light-cure time)
9. Final restoration (composite resin, polishing)
10. Radiograph (post-operative, intraoral PA)

### RANDOMIZATION TIMING
**Critical**: Occurs AFTER restoration, BEFORE occlusal management

**GROUP 1 (TEST)**: Occlusal reduction to 0% relative force (T-Scan monitored)
**GROUP 2 (CONTROL)**: Sham procedure (simulates grinding, verifies no force change)

### POST-OPERATIVE MEDICATION
**Analgesic**: Ibuprofen 600mg
**Prescription**: 10 tablets
**Instructions**: Use as-needed for moderate-severe pain (NRS >6)

## POST-OPERATIVE MONITORING

### TELEPHONIC CONSULTATION SCHEDULE
- **0 hours** (at dismissal)
- **6th hour**
- **12th hour**
- **24 hours** (1st day)
- **2nd through 7th day** (daily, same time each day)

**Purpose**: Pain assessment + analgesic tracking
**Emergency Protocol**: Severe pain → contact operator, additional tablet, office visit
**Failed Case Trigger**: Pain uncontrolled after intervention → Convert to pulpectomy

### ANALGESIC CONSUMPTION SCALE (3-point)
| Score | Pain Level | Medication Effect |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|
| 0 | No/Mild | No medication needed |
| 1 | Moderate | Controlled, no interference |
| 2 | Severe | Uncontrolled, interferes with life |

### CLINICAL RECALL VISITS
- **1st day**: Pain + percussion sensitivity assessment
- **7th day**: Pain + percussion sensitivity assessment
- **3rd month**: Clinical evaluation
- **6th month**: Clinical evaluation + GROUP 1 ONLY (prosthetic rehabilitation)
- **12th month**: Clinical evaluation

### RADIOGRAPHIC RECALL VISITS
- **6th month**: Intraoral PA radiograph
- **12th month**: Intraoral PA radiograph

## OUTCOME ASSESSMENT DEFINITIONS

### SUCCESS CRITERIA
**Clinical**:
- Patient asymptomatic
- No swelling or sinus tract
- Negative percussion/palpation response
- Positive cold test response

**Radiographic**:
- Normal periapical architecture
- No root resorption
- No pulp canal obliteration

### FAILURE CRITERIA
**Clinical**:
- Symptomatic tooth
- Presence of swelling/sinus tract
- Positive percussion/palpation response
- Negative cold test response

**Radiographic**:
- Widened PDL space
- Apical/furcal radiolucency
- Pulp canal obliterations
- Internal resorption

## FORMATTING CONVENTIONS

**Typography**:
- **Bold**: Section headings, key terms, drug names
- **Underline**: Major section titles in protocol
- **Italics**: Terminology, technical terms
- **Superscript**: Reference numbers (^1^, ^2^, etc.)

**Lists**:
- Numbered (1, 2, 3...): Procedures, inclusion/exclusion criteria when listed sequentially
- Bulleted: Criteria, equipment items, evaluation parameters

**Tables**:
- Used for: Inclusion/exclusion criteria, pain characteristics, outcome assessment, analgesic scoring
- Borders: Standard table borders with clear headers
- Alignment: Left-aligned text, centered headers

**Spacing**:
- Single or 1.5 line spacing (typical)
- 1-inch margins all sides
- Page numbers at bottom
- Section breaks between major sections

## RED FLAGS TO AVOID

- [ ] Incomplete inclusion/exclusion criteria
- [ ] Missing CTRI registration number
- [ ] English PIS/ICF without certified Hindi translations
- [ ] Vague outcome definitions (use specific clinical/radiographic criteria)
- [ ] No baseline pain intensity threshold (should be NRS ≥4 or similar)
- [ ] Inadequate haemostasis time specification (should be 8-minute limit)
- [ ] Missing equipment specifications (manufacturer, model numbers)
- [ ] No interoperative exclusion criteria
- [ ] Incomplete data collection forms
- [ ] No contact information for investigator/supervisor
- [ ] Missing safety measures section
- [ ] No regulatory checklist (DCGI, DGFT status)
- [ ] Incomplete follow-up schedule
- [ ] Vague statistical analysis plan
- [ ] No mention of ethical clearance process

## FINAL REPLICATION NOTES

**Word Count**: Nisarg's protocol is approximately 40,000+ words across 60+ pages
**Development Time**: Expect 3-4 weeks for quality protocol writing
**Key Differentiator**: Specific research gap identification and strong literature justification
**Critical Success Factor**: Bilingual participant documentation with certified translations
**IEC Review Timeline**: Typically 4-8 weeks after submission for ethical approval

**Best Practices**:
1. Follow AIIMS template structure exactly
2. Ensure all IEC form sections are populated
3. Use specific, measurable outcome definitions
4. Include contemporary literature (last 5-10 years)
5. Detail all equipment with manufacturers and model numbers
6. Provide bilingual documentation with certification
7. Be explicit about inclusion/exclusion criteria
8. Define success/failure clearly with objective criteria
9. Include all data collection forms as appendices
10. Obtain CTRI registration before or during IEC submission
