## Article 2: A Pragmatic Randomized Controlled Trial of Ambient Artificial Intelligence to Improve Health Practitioner Well-Being

### Citation Information
- **PMC ID**: PMC12858090
- **PMID**: 41625485
- **DOI**: [10.1056/aioa2500945](https://doi.org/10.1056/aioa2500945)
- **Journal**: NEJM AI
- **Study Type**: Pragmatic RCT with stepped-wedge design

### Methodological Summary

#### Study Design
Pragmatic Randomized Controlled Trial (Stepped-Wedge Design)

#### Sample Size and Calculation
- **Total Sample**: 66 healthcare practitioners across ambulatory clinics in two states; 71,487 notes analyzed (27,092 with AI)
- **Sample Calculation Method**: Individually randomized to three 6-week sequences over 24-week trial period; stratified stepped-wedge design

#### Primary Outcome Measures
Professional fulfillment and work exhaustion/interpersonal disengagement from Stanford Professional Fulfillment Index (5-point Likert scale)

#### Secondary Outcomes/Measures
(See primary outcomes section for comprehensive outcome framework)

#### Data Collection Methods
24-week stepped-wedge individually randomized pragmatic trial; real-world ambulatory clinic setting; automatic note generation and practitioner feedback

#### Assessment Instruments & Tools
Stanford Professional Fulfillment Index; Provider Documentation Summarization Quality Instrument (PDSQI-9); time tracking for notes and work-outside-work

#### Statistical Methods Employed
Linear mixed models for intention-to-treat (ITT) analysis; 95% confidence intervals calculated; p-values reported

#### Key Quantitative Findings
Work exhaustion/disengagement reduction: -0.44 points (95% CI -0.62 to -0.25; p<0.001); Professional fulfillment: +0.14 points (95% CI 0.004 to 0.28; p=0.04); Time on notes decreased: -0.36 hours/day (95% CI -0.55); 38% of notes generated using ambient AI

## Article 4: Comparing a tablet computer and paper forms for assessing patient-reported outcomes in edentulous patients

### Citation Information
- **PMC ID**: PMC5179484
- **PMID**: 28018563
- **DOI**: [10.4047/jap.2016.8.6.457](https://doi.org/10.4047/jap.2016.8.6.457)
- **Journal**: The Journal of Advanced Prosthodontics
- **Study Type**: Comparative cross-sectional with counterbalanced design

### Methodological Summary

#### Study Design
Comparative Cross-sectional Study (Within-subjects design)

#### Sample Size and Calculation
- **Total Sample**: 30 edentulous patients from university clinic
- **Sample Calculation Method**: Cross-over design; 30 participants completing both methods with one-month interval between assessments

#### Primary Outcome Measures
Comparability of patient-reported outcomes (PRO) between tablet and paper forms; time required for each method

#### Secondary Outcomes/Measures
(See primary outcomes section for comprehensive outcome framework)

#### Data Collection Methods
Two questionnaires: (1) Denture satisfaction (100-mm VAS), (2) OHIP-EDENT; both administered via tablet and paper in random order

#### Assessment Instruments & Tools
Denture satisfaction questionnaire (100-mm Visual Analog Scale); OHIP-EDENT (Oral Health Impact Profile for Edentulous patients)

#### Statistical Methods Employed
Paired t-tests for time comparison; internal consistency assessment (Cronbach's α); correlation and agreement analysis (moderate to high correlation expected)

#### Key Quantitative Findings
Similar mean results between methods for all denture satisfaction items and OHIP-EDENT summary score; Good internal consistency both methods (Cronbach's α ≥ 0.86); Electronic: 9.2 min, Paper: 8.5 min (p=0.06, non-significant); Moderate to good correlation/agreement between methods

## Cross-Article Methodology Patterns Relevant to Protocol Development

### Common Study Designs Across Literature
1. **Clinical Audits** (PMC8066672): Retrospective analysis of treatment records with predefined quality criteria
2. **Pragmatic RCTs** (PMC12858090): Real-world testing of AI interventions with clinical outcomes
3. **Usability/Acceptability Studies** (PMC12829107, PMC5179484): Cross-sectional evaluation of tool feasibility
4. **Framework Development Studies** (PMC12700715): Survey-based conceptual model creation
5. **Proof-of-Concept Protocols** (PMC12012399): Structured testing of new technology implementation

### Recommended Instruments for Endodontic Documentation Protocol
- **System Usability Scale (SUS)**: Standardized 10-item tool for technology assessment
- **Customized Audit Tools**: Based on clinical evidence-based criteria (as used in PMC8066672)
- **Time-Motion Analysis**: Quantify documentation burden (PMC12700715, PMC12012399)
- **Professional Burden Scales**: Measure clinician burnout and satisfaction (PMC12858090)
- **Acceptability Framework**: Evaluate implementation feasibility (PMC12829107)

### Sample Size Considerations
- **Clinical Audits**: 50-200 cases/teeth recommended (PMC8066672)
- **Usability Studies**: 30 participants per group/site (PMC12829107)
- **Comparative Studies**: 30+ cross-over participants (PMC5179484)
- **RCTs**: 60+ practitioners for powered studies (PMC12858090)

### Statistical Analysis Approaches
- **Descriptive Statistics**: Appropriate for exploratory audit and usability studies
- **Inferential Analysis**: Linear mixed models for pragmatic RCTs with multiple timepoints (PMC12858090)
- **Thematic Analysis**: Qualitative component for acceptability/usability studies
- **Factor Analysis**: For framework development (PMC12700715)
- **Correlation/Agreement Analysis**: For comparative validity studies (PMC5179484)

### Documentation Time Metrics
- Benchmark from literature: 36 minutes charting per 30 minutes clinical time (PMC12700715)
- Electronic vs. paper comparison: No significant difference (8.5-9.2 minutes per questionnaire, PMC5179484)
- Expected reduction with AI assistance: 0.36 hours per day (PMC12858090)

### Quality Assessment Frameworks
- **Endodontic Treatment Quality**: Vitality testing, intracanal medicament documentation, radiographic assessment (PMC8066672)
- **Documentation Quality**: Provider Documentation Summarization Quality Instrument (PDSQI-9) (PMC12858090)
- **System Usability**: System Usability Scale and Framework for Acceptability (PMC12829107)
